What forms of payment do you accept?

We make checkout easy with a wide range of payment options: E-Transfer auto deposit (our preferred method), PayPal, credit card, Google Pay, and Apple Pay. Prefer to spread out the cost? We also offer buy now, pay later through AfterPay & Humm, with 0 fees and 0% interest, so you can take your new appliance home today and pay over time. Want to see if you qualify or learn more about flexible payment plans? Visit our Financing page for full details.

When is payment due for your order?

With buy now, pay later, you can get your appliance today without paying a cent today — payments are split into manageable installments through AfterPay. If you're paying by credit card, PayPal, Google Pay, or Apple Pay, payment is simply made at checkout. If you choose E-Transfer Auto Deposit, payment is due within 2 hours of placing your order to keep your delivery or pickup on schedule.
